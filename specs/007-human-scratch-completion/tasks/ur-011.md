## Root ANSWERED the question this task told you to establish first

The task said to determine whether the table receives the DISPLAYED slice or the full filtered list
before designing anything. Root traced it, and the answer is the one that makes the principal's
report a real defect. **Verify it yourself** — several of root's readings have been corrected by
workers in this goal, two of them in the immediately preceding package.

The chain:

- `TransactionTable.tsx:274` derives
  `const filteredIds = useMemo(() => transactions.map((t) => t.id), [transactions])` — so
  `filteredIds` is exactly whatever the `transactions` PROP contains.
- `src/app/(app)/transactions/page.tsx:1172` passes `transactions={tableData}`.
- `tableData` at `:360-362` is `displayedTransactions.map(...)`.
- `displayedTransactions` is `filteredTransactions.slice(0, displayCount)` with `PAGE_SIZE = 50`.

**So `filteredIds` contains ONLY the currently loaded page.** Select-all therefore selects at most
`displayCount` rows, not every row matching the filters, and the header checkbox's own
checked/indeterminate state is computed over the same partial set. Both halves of the principal's
report are explained.

**This makes the efficiency clause load-bearing rather than decorative.** The obvious fix — pass the
full `filteredTransactions` to the table — would materialise every matching id and re-render the
whole table's memo dependency on every page extension, which is exactly what the frozen text forbids
at a hundred thousand transactions. Root is NOT prescribing the shape: a materialised `Set` and a
select-all-with-exceptions model are both defensible, and the frozen text requires you to justify
the choice against the stated scale and MEASURE it rather than assert it. But whichever you choose
must not make `filteredIds` the vehicle, because that prop is also what drives rendering.

Note the interaction with UR-010 (P31): `toggleRow`'s shift-range uses `filteredIds.indexOf(...)` to
find range endpoints. If you change what that array contains, you change what a shift-range spans.
The two packages touch the same hook, so whichever lands second must re-verify the other's behaviour
rather than assume it.

## Required behaviour

Rule from the frozen text. In summary: the header checkbox selects every transaction matching the
active filters including unrendered and not-yet-paged rows; clearing clears that same set; the
checkbox reflects the whole filtered set as selected, indeterminate or clear; changing filters
re-derives that set; and bulk actions afterwards apply to every selected transaction.

**Efficiency is part of the requirement, not a nice-to-have.** Selecting all must not force rows to
render, must not page in rows merely to enumerate them, and must stay responsive at a hundred
thousand transactions. Neither the selection nor the header state may be derived by a scan costing
rendered-rows x matching-rows. Consider whether a materialised `Set` of every matching id is the
right representation at that scale, or whether a select-all-with-exceptions model serves better;
justify the choice in evidence against the stated scale rather than by assertion, and measure it.

Keep the hook pure and its data immutable.
